Ongoing research

Continual learning for VLA

Retaining capabilities while learning new robotic tasks.

Continual learningLIBEROSO-101

My role   Research participant; research questions and method exploration.

Which parts of past experience should a robot reuse when learning a new task?

Research context

The project considers continual robot learning with the LIBERO simulation environment and the SO-101 robot. Areas of investigation include differences between larger foundation models and lightweight policies, retention of earlier tasks, and the resource costs of replay.

My focus

I am interested in the relationship between preserving past capabilities and the cost of retaining and revisiting experience. I have also been exploring shared representations with task-specific composition and selective capacity expansion as research hypotheses.

Questions under investigation

  • How does experience selection affect learning and retention under a fixed budget?
  • When can an existing representation be reused for a new task?
  • How should task success, forgetting, storage, and additional parameters be evaluated together?
