Project

Maestro / MobileKT

Personalized knowledge tracing on mobile devices.

Knowledge tracingModel designOn-device AI

My role   Team lead · MobileKT model architecture · on-device deployment.

How can a compact model represent a learner’s changing knowledge state?

The project

Maestro combines quiz generation and knowledge tracing in a mobile study assistant. MobileKT builds on MIKT and uses a content-based question representation and a Time-Aware Probe to estimate concept mastery. The system combines server-side components with on-device knowledge-state updates.

My contribution

I served as team lead and contributed to the MobileKT architecture and on-device deployment. This connected model design with the practical constraints of integrating a knowledge-tracing component into a mobile application.

Reported implementation results

The team’s final report records a total size of 1.48 MB for the bundled on-device ONNX models. Across 63 measured steps on a Galaxy Tab S8, the median knowledge-tracing step latency was 49 ms. These figures describe the on-device knowledge-tracing components, not the entire app or server-assisted response time.

What I take forward

This project informs my interest in persistent state representations, personalization, and the trade-offs between predictive performance, interpretability, and deployment cost. Knowledge-state updates here are distinct from continual updates to model weights.
